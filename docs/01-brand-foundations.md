# Brand foundations

## Identity

Use the product name **TEMBUS**. The approved promise is **Lebih Dekat, Lebih Cepat**.

Service descriptors such as `TEMBUS Paket`, `TEMBUS Food`, or `TEMBUS Towing` may be used when they clarify a navigation or campaign context. They do not replace the master brand.

## Brand roles

| Role | Meaning | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest green | Trust, operations, continuity | Headers, navigation, hero surfaces, primary brand areas |
| Emerald | Positive action and progress | Primary interactive states, active navigation, success progress |
| Orange | Urgency and action | Main CTA, selected campaign emphasis, critical operational cue |
| Neutral | Legibility and calm | Page backgrounds, cards, documents, tables |

Orange is an emphasis color, not a full-page background. On a typical functional screen it should remain a small portion of the visual area.

## Logo usage

Approved working assets are in `assets/brand/`:

- `tembus-logo-white.png` for forest-green or dark surfaces.
- `tembus-mark-color.png` for small brand-mark contexts where the available artwork is sufficient.

Use the logo with generous clear space. Never stretch, rotate, add a drop shadow, redraw the mark, or recolor it without a documented brand review. Do not place the white logo on a light surface without a sufficient dark container.

For production print or high-resolution campaigns, replace working PNGs with the approved vector master when the brand team provides one. Do not recreate the logo from a screenshot.

## Photography and illustration

Use imagery to explain the service or establish trust: delivery in motion, real operational context, clear route or destination cues. Avoid decorative imagery on payment failure, emergency assistance, active courier jobs, or dense admin control screens.

Image treatment:

- Keep the main subject clear of text and controls.
- Use a dark forest gradient behind text placed on an image.
- Do not cover important operational information with a decorative overlay.
- For customer-facing marketing, imagery may be expressive; for operations, clarity wins.

## Naming and copy

Write in clear Indonesian by default. Use sentence case, active verbs, and specific next actions. Avoid unexplained internal abbreviations. Use the same product terms across apps, admin, reports, and support materials.

Preferred examples:

- `Lacak Paket`
- `Dalam Perjalanan`
- `Coba Lagi`
- `Hubungi Bantuan`

Avoid:

- `Track Now!!!`
- `Process Failed` when the user needs an actionable Indonesian message.
- All-caps paragraphs or marketing copy that hides the action.
